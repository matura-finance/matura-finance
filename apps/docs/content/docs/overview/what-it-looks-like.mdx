The Matura app lives at [`app.usematura.xyz`](https://app.usematura.xyz). You connect a crypto wallet
(on the BNB Chain test network), and the app is organized into three pages you switch between from the
top navigation bar: **Vaults**, **Portfolio**, and **Issuer Demo**. Matura never touches your wallet's
secret key — when an action needs a signature, your own wallet asks you to approve it.

Here's what each page is for.

## Portfolio

Your home base — everything about *your* money lives here, split into two tabs: **Positions** and
**Activity**.

### Positions

Your balance and your claims — the income you're owed, how much of each you've already turned into
cash, and what's still available to finance.

![Portfolio Positions — wallet balance and a summary of your verified future payments.](/app/02-portfolio.png)

Each claim shows its state (attested → eligible → funded → paid), its face value, and how much is
still **available to route** for liquidity.

![Your claims, each with its state, face value, maturity, and available-to-route amount.](/app/03-portfolio.png)

From here, the **Get Liquidity** button starts the financing flow: you enter how much you need, and
Matura compares your eligible claims against the competing vaults and finds the cheapest route —
*review → sign → confirm*, and the cash is yours.

![Get Liquidity — enter the amount you need and Matura finds your best route across the vaults.](/app/05-get-liquidity.png)

### Activity

Your history: every financing and settlement event, in order, each linking to the transaction on the
public block explorer (BscScan) so anyone can verify it happened.

![Portfolio Activity — the on-chain event ledger with a BscScan link per transaction.](/app/04-porfolio-activity.png)

## Vaults

The lenders' side. Each vault is a pool of capital with its own mandate (which claim types it accepts)
and pricing. This page compares them — the choices Matura's best-execution engine picks between on your
behalf.

![The Vaults page — comparing each vault's supported claims, liquidity, and pricing.](/app/01-vaullts.png)

## Issuer Demo

A demo tool. In the real world, an employer or platform would vouch for your income by signing a
digital attestation. For this testnet demo, the Issuer Demo page lets you play that role yourself — so
you can create a claim and drive the whole flow end to end.

![The Issuer Simulator — stand in for an issuer to drive the demo from issued to financed to settled.](/app/06-demo-issuer.png)

It lists the claims you've issued, which you can settle (or mark delayed) once they mature.

![Your issued claims, with their status and settle/delay actions.](/app/07-demo-issuer.png)
