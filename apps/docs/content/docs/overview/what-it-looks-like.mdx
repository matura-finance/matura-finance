title: What it looks like
description: A tour of the Matura product surface — the five routes of the wallet-connected app.
---

The product app (`app.usematura.xyz`) is a wagmi + viem Next.js app that connects a wallet on **BNB
Smart Chain Testnet only**. It never holds your key: every on-chain action is prepared server-side as
unsigned calldata or EIP-712 typed data, which your wallet signs locally. Five routes cover the full
flow.

## `/request` — the best-execution flow

The core screen. You pick a claim to finance; the app calls the router's `optimize` endpoint, shows
the **cheapest verifiable route** across competing vaults (with a per-leg breakdown and the total you
receive), then walks you through **review → sign → submit → index**. The signed message is the single
source of truth: the same coerced payload feeds the signature, the on-chain hash, and `executeRoute`.

## `/account`

Your position overview — wallet balances, claims you own, and their state (registered, financed,
settled). Reads come from the API's projection of on-chain events, so they reflect indexed finality.

## `/activity`

A chronological ledger of your executions and settlements, each linking to its on-chain transaction on
BscScan. This is the "proof it happened" view.

## `/vaults`

The liquidity side: each vault's mandate, available capital, and pricing. Vaults compete to fund
claims; this screen shows the supply the router chooses between.

## `/issuer`

A demo simulator for the issuer role — it produces EIP-712 claim attestations so you can drive the end
-to-end flow on testnet without a real payroll/invoice integration.
