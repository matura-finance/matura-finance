The Matura app lives at [`app.usematura.xyz`](https://app.usematura.xyz). You connect a crypto wallet
(on the BNB Chain test network), and from then on the app is organized into a few pages you switch
between. Matura never touches your wallet's secret key — when an action needs a signature, your own
wallet asks you to approve it.

Here's what each page is for.

## Portfolio

Your home base — everything about *your* money lives here, split into two views:

### Position

Your claims and balances: the income you're owed, how much of each claim you've already turned into
cash, and what's still available. This is where you **turn a claim into cash** — Matura finds you the
best deal across the competing vaults, shows you exactly how much you'll receive, you review it, your
wallet signs, and the money is yours.

### Activity

Your history: every financing request and every repayment, in order, each with a link to the public
blockchain record (BscScan) so anyone can verify it actually happened.

## Vaults

The lenders' side. Each vault is a pool of money with its own rules and prices. This page shows what
each vault offers — the choices Matura's best-deal engine picks between on your behalf when you finance
a claim from your Position.

{/* screenshot: Vaults page — /screens/vaults.png */}

## Issuer Demo

A demo tool. In the real world, an employer or platform would vouch for your income by signing a
digital attestation. For this testnet demo, the Issuer Demo page lets you play that role yourself, so
you can create a claim and try the whole flow end to end.
