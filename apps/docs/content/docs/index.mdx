**Matura turns money you've already earned — but haven't been paid yet — into cash today.**

Think of an invoice due next month, salary you've accrued before payday, or a payment stream that
vests over time. That's real value you own, but you can't spend it yet. Matura lets you get most of it
now, at a fair, transparent price, without handing your funds to anyone.

It does this on the **BNB Smart Chain test network**. When you ask for cash, several lenders compete,
and Matura automatically picks the **cheapest combination** and pays you — then proves that choice was
honored, on the blockchain, where anyone can check it.

> **Testnet only.** This is an early version running on a test network with pretend money. No real
> funds are ever involved.

## A few ideas up front

- **Claim** — the future income you want to cash in (an invoice, wages, a stream).
- **Vault** — a pool of money that lends against claims. Several compete for your business.
- **Best-execution** — Matura always picks the cheapest verifiable offer, and the blockchain enforces
  it. You don't have to trust that you got a fair deal; you can verify it.
- **Non-custodial** — Matura never holds your wallet's key. You approve every action yourself.
